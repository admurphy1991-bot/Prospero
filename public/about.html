Our mission

Manuals that act. Registers that live.

Every building is handed over with everything needed to look after it, then it goes on a shelf. Our mission is to make every O&M manual actionable and to make the static asset register a thing of the past, so the people responsible for a building always know what's due, why, and where it came from.

What we believe

Paperwork doesn't maintain buildings.

People do. They need a plan they'll actually use, not 4,000 pages they'll never open.

A register should be live.

It should update when an asset is replaced, a task is done or a contractor changes, not once a year in a spreadsheet.

Show your working.

Every task links back to the page it came from, so anyone can check it, from facilities managers to auditors and buyers.

Who we are

A small Auckland team that's been on both sides of the handover.

We come from construction, property and software. We've watched good buildings slip because the information to look after them was sitting in a box, a drive or a council file.

Prospero is the tool we wanted on every one of those jobs.
