The problem

Buildings are handed over.
The documentation isn't.

Every building's maintenance obligations are on paper somewhere: in the handover manuals for a new build, in council files and contractor records for an older one. Almost none of it is acted on systematically.

Time

40–80 hours per building

Extracting maintenance tasks from a single building's documentation consumes specialist time that should be spent on risk-critical work, not reading PDFs.

Coverage

BWoF covers the regulated minimum

Facades, membranes and envelope systems sit outside the compliance scope entirely, with no plan and no visibility.

Scale

No compounding benefit

Each new acquisition restarts the same manual process from scratch. No institutional memory, no consistency as a portfolio grows.

Liability

Deferred maintenance deteriorates

When issues surface, remediation typically costs 3–7× what proactive maintenance would have. The longer it's deferred, the worse the outcome.

The compliance gap

BWoF covers the regulated minimum. The assets that drive your largest costs and liabilities sit entirely outside it, unmanaged by default.

That gap is where deferred maintenance liability builds up silently, until it surfaces. Prospero shows the full picture: structured, traceable, complete.

Product

Everything that was specified. Nothing left behind.

Four views into a live maintenance programme, generated directly from your own building documentation rather than a template.

Asset register

A complete asset register, not just a task list

Every asset in the building is catalogued down to the tag, location and serial number, with the lifecycle data your capital plan actually needs.

  • Expected useful life and installed date tracked per asset, not per building
  • Condition, quantity and estimated replacement cost roll up into a portfolio-wide capex forecast
  • Search any asset type, tag or location across every property in seconds
Compliance & warranty tagging

Every warranty and compliance obligation, scheduled

Tasks are pulled from manufacturer specifications with the correct frequency already set, and split cleanly by what's legally required and what protects your warranty.

  • Frequencies (quarterly, biannual, as-needed) set automatically from the source document
  • Compliance and Warranty tags applied to every task, with next-due dates and one-click completion
  • Full history retained against each task for audit and handover
Traceable to source

Tasks tied to your Specified Systems

Each task carries its Specified System tag, so compliance items line up directly against the systems your IQPs are accountable for, and every entry cites the exact page it came from.

  • SS-number tagging maps straight onto your building's compliance schedule
  • Add, edit or remove tasks per asset type as scope or contractors change
  • Every task links back to a page reference in its source document
New · 30-year LTMP

A long-term maintenance plan that updates itself

Replacement costs roll up from the asset register into a 30-year forecast, inflated and tested against your sinking fund. When an asset changes, the plan changes with it.

  • Set annual contributions, opening balance and inflation, then solve for no annual shortfall
  • Break the forecast down by discipline: mechanical, electrical, fire, façade, vertical transport and more
  • Every replacement line traces back to its asset, install date, condition and source
