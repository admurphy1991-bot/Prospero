New — Lifecycle forecasting

A complete asset register, not just a task list

Every asset in the building is catalogued — down to the tag, location and serial number — with the lifecycle data your capital plan actually needs.

  • Expected useful life and installed date tracked per asset, not per building
  • Condition, quantity and estimated replacement cost roll up into a portfolio-wide capex forecast
  • Search any asset type, tag or location across every property in seconds
Compliance & warranty tagging

Every warranty and compliance obligation, scheduled

Tasks are pulled from manufacturer specifications with the correct frequency already set — and split cleanly by what's legally required and what protects your warranty.

  • Frequencies — quarterly, biannual, as-needed — set automatically from the source manual
  • Compliance and Warranty tags applied to every task, with next-due dates and one-click completion
  • Full history retained against each task for audit and handover
Traceable to source

Tasks tied to your Specified Systems

Each task carries its Specified System tag, so compliance items line up directly against the systems your IQPs are accountable for — and every entry cites the exact page it came from.

  • SS-number tagging maps straight onto your building's compliance schedule
  • Add, edit or remove tasks per asset type as scope or contractors change
  • Every task links back to a page reference in the source O&M manual
